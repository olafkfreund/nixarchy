# Plan: Hyprland and its GPU drivers come from the same nixpkgs

## Approved decisions (self-contained)

- **Mesa from Hyprland's nixpkgs.** Inside `config = lib.mkIf cfg.enable` in
  `modules/nixos.nix`, set:
  - `hardware.graphics.package = lib.mkDefault hyprPkgs.mesa`;
  - `hardware.graphics.package32 = lib.mkDefault hyprPkgs.pkgsi686Linux.mesa`;
  - where `hyprPkgs = inputs.hyprland.inputs.nixpkgs.legacyPackages.${pkgs.stdenv.hostPlatform.system}`.

  This is hyprwm's documented setup. Hyprland keeps its own nixpkgs and its
  cache. A user's own setting wins.
- **`checks.graphics-glibc` (new, evaluation-only):** it fails when the glibc
  of `hardware.graphics.package`, or of `package32` when `enable32Bit` is on,
  is newer than that of `programs.hyprland.package`, the compositor the module
  starts (`modules/nixos.nix:982`).
  - It cannot have a permanent negative control: on `main` both are glibc
    2.42.
  - §1 is shown once, on #1154's lock.
- **Docs:** `docs/internals/flake.md`'s hyprland section, and
  `modules/AGENTS.md` if it lists system-wide settings.
- **The proof on #1154** comes from a throwaway branch with #1154's lock plus
  this change, whose CI `checks.session` must go green.

## Deviations (2026-10-02)

- **Step 1: `lib.mkOverride 900`, not `lib.mkDefault`.**
  `nixos/modules/hardware/graphics.nix` sets `package`/`package32` at
  `mkDefault` itself once graphics is enabled, and a second `mkDefault` fails
  with "defined multiple times" (reproduced). At 900, a user's plain
  assignment still wins, but a user's own `mkDefault` no longer does. The
  docs in step 4 say so.
- **Step 2's §1 scratch copy:** `cp -a` of a linked worktree copies its `.git`
  pointer file, so the copy shared the real worktree's index and staged
  #1154's lock there. Caught and repaired: `flake.lock` equals HEAD in both
  the index and the tree. Recorded in AGENTS.md §5.
- **Review of #1162:**
  - The check now covers `package32` always, and every `extraPackages(32)`
    entry with a C toolchain. The latter is p620's case. Shown red with a fake
    glibc-2.44 `extraPackages` entry against a 2.42 compositor.
  - The docs' closure figure is corrected to about 1.1 GB (`path-info -S`);
    4.05 GB summed per-path closures.
  - The docs now say an overlay on `mesa` no longer reaches the drivers.
