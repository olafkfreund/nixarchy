# Plan: Let MicroVM mutations wait out brief status probes

Refs #1098. Change only the shared mutation path in `nixarchy-vm`: `lock_stopped_vm` waits up to two seconds (`flock -w 2`) for its existing exclusive `.lock`, then keeps the exact `nixarchy-vm: '<name>' is already running.` refusal for a real long-lived holder. This covers `run`, `run --detach`, `set-template`, and `rm` through their shared helper. Keep the runner's lifetime lock, `launch_vm`'s distinct `-w 30` handoff, `unit_busy`'s active/activating/reloading refusal, both `flock -n` status probes in `pkgs/microvm.nix` and `pkgs/verify.sh`, and the text/JSON `list` shape. `stop` does not take this lock. #1097 separately tracks CI hygiene; this item is **Refs #1098**, not a closure of the batch issue. No workflow, VM boot, or Mode A change.

The wait cannot deadlock with a status probe: `lock_held` (`pkgs/microvm.nix:158-166`) and the verifier (`pkgs/verify.sh:683-694`) close fd 8 immediately after their nonblocking test, with no wait while holding it. The mutation holds fd 9 while checking `unit_busy`, which calls systemd rather than waiting for a probe. `detach_vm` closes fd 9 before its readiness poll. All `-n` callers were inspected: the two probes, `lock_stopped_vm`, and the external-holder fixtures at `tests/microvm-template.nix:692-693,862-863`. All non-mutation uses stay as they are.
