## Rollback

Never by resetting `edge-candidate` or `edge` (§11). A carry on
`edge-candidate` is kept by `edge-carry.sh` until it lands or is undone, so
it is undone forward:

- **Before promotion** (verify failed, or a problem found in review): a
  `git revert` of the carry's commits on `edge-candidate`, flake.lock
  included, pushed as a fast-forward, then a `from_candidate=true` dispatch
  that verifies edge's previous pin again.
- **After promotion** (edge users have it): the same forward revert, through
  `edge-candidate` and a dispatch, so `edge` moves only once verify passes
  on the reverted tree. Users then roll back locally with
  `nixos-rebuild --rollback` until the next edge build reaches them.
