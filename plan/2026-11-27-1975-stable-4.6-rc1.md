# Plan: stable moves from Omarchy v4.5.0 to v4.6.0-rc1

Revision 2, route (b). Revision 1's plan was reviewed by Codex and agy, who
found it could not hold under two quattro pins. Their findings are folded in.

## Approved decisions, carried over

- **Phase 1 is a carry on `edge-candidate`.** It pins quattro
  `745271ccd293fc2e7a233b269004a9926a5c6cb4` (rc1's commit). It is pushed and
  dispatched with `from_candidate=true`, never while an edge run is in
  flight (§8). Worktree: `/mnt/data/vmtest/wt-1975e`, branch
  `fix/1975-edge-rc1` off `origin/edge`, fast-forward pushed to
  `edge-candidate`.
- **Phase 2 runs on `chore/1975-stable-4.6-rc1`** (`/mnt/data/vmtest/wt-1975`).
  It merges `main`, merges `edge` for real, pins `v4.6.0-rc1` (rev
  `745271c…`, narHash `sha256-0tGp3frcYptp7b40nb8UO+mcol3JZTm1UZYUUxC8jio=`),
  and adds the predicate. Merge with `gh pr merge --merge`.
- **Predicate:** `lib: version: lib.hasPrefix "edge-" version ||
  lib.versionAtLeast version "4.6.0"`. Not `"4.6.0-rc1"`, because Nix sorts
  final 4.6.0 below rc1. `installer/cd.nix`'s ISO label keeps its own
  `hasPrefix "edge-"`.
- **Fingerprint:** the old `setup_pam_config` path is chosen by the
  function's presence in the file, never by pin. The PAM check requires an
  active `auth` line naming exactly `pam_fprintd.so` (bare or as a store path).
  The enroll helper keeps the caller's `PATH` and `OMARCHY_PATH` and uses
  the fprintd preflight instead of pacman.
- **Ledgers stay two-state:** quattro (rc1's content) against main's 4.5 tag.
- **No workflow edit**; if one is needed, stop (§11). Implementers never stage,
  commit, push, build on p510, deploy or switch; the lead does all of that.
- Do not push to `update/omarchy-v4.6.0-rc1`; close #1972 after Phase 2.
