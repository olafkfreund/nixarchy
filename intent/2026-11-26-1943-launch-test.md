# Intent: a bumped app is launch-tested, so the bump merges itself

Approved by the owner's standing pre-approval and "start on #1943 and #1946" (2026-11-26).

## Problem

The daily bump PR is not auto-merged because an app can build and crash at launch, so the owner launches every changed app by hand (#1936).

## Proposed outcome

CI launches each changed app; a complete, passing, version-and-hash-only bump merges itself after the required checks.

## Affected users and systems

update.yml and its PRs.

## Constraints

No p620 install slot; no secret in the launch job; §8 skipped-passes trap; a human merges the workflow change (§11).
