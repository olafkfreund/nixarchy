## Rollback

`edge` keeps its last good pin until the candidate's run is green, so a
failed carry changes nothing users get. To undo after publishing:

1. dispatch the edge workflow with the previous pin (`74de104`) on a
   reverted `edge-candidate`;
2. the hosts follow at their next bump.

Nothing here touches `main`.
