# Intent: the Shure MV7+ udev rule reaches a NixOS system

## Problem

Omarchy v4.6.0-rc1 supports the Shure MV7+ microphone's onboard DSP over hidraw.
Its migration 1795709363 installs `default/udev/shure-mv7plus.rules` into
`/etc/udev/rules.d` and adds the `omarchy.mv7plus` bar widget. NixOS generates
`/etc/udev/rules.d`, so the migration is declined (#1975) and no rule lands.
Without the rule's `TAG+="uaccess"`, the logged-in user cannot open the
device's hidraw node, and the widget rc1 ships in its default `shell.json`
cannot reach the DSP.

## Proposed outcome

On a pin that ships the rule, a nixarchy system carries it in its udev rules
(sorted before `73-seat-late.rules`, as upstream intends), and a check fails
if it is missing.

## Affected users and systems

Anyone with an MV7+ on a quattro or rc1 pin. Nothing changes on the 4.5 tag,
which has no rule.

## Constraints

Mode A: nothing new for a user who imports the module and does not use the
Omarchy desktop. Hold under both pins. No `/etc/udev/rules.d` writes.

## Open questions

None.
