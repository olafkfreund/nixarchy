# Intent: hosts follow a green edge, with rollback

Approved by the owner: "start on #1946", and the scope choice below (2026-11-26).

Owner decision (2026-11-26, "Phased"): build all of it, enable only the nixos_config bump and razer's unattended switch. p620's unattended switch is built but stays OFF (an option defaulting to false) until the runner-drain test the design names passes; turning it on is a later one-line change by the owner.

## Problem

Every edge release reaches p620 and razer by hand: bump, build, merge, announce, switch, verify.

## Proposed outcome

A green, promoted edge commit is bumped into nixos_config and switched onto razer unattended, with a health check and automatic rollback; p620 follows once its runners can be drained safely.

## Affected users and systems

nixarchy (omarchy-edge.yml publishes the promoted SHA), nixos_config (bump workflow, upgrade module), razer, later p620.

## Constraints

No switch while a CI job runs on p620; razer only on AC; a regression rolls back and pauses; a human merges workflow changes (§11).
