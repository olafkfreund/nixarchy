# Plan: quattro's new /usr references stop the edge channel publishing

Approved decisions, carried from the spec:

- All three references are harmless on NixOS. None is patched.
  - `omarchy-install-t3code-app`: `usr/` inside the extracted AppImage, and a
    string compare with an Arch-made `~/.local/bin/t3` link target.
  - `omarchy-remove-ai-t3-code`: `/usr/bin/t3` strings as `case` patterns.
  - `omarchy-hw-fingerprint`: an Apple-Silicon platform file read behind
    `-r`, which cannot exist on x86_64 NixOS, so detection is the USB scan.
- One edit: three named exceptions with reasons in `tests/session.nix`'s
  `/usr` guard `allowed` list. They land on `main`, where the names are inert,
  and reach edge at its next merge of `main`. Nothing is carried on `edge`.
- Whether the t3code AppImage works on NixOS is out of scope, to be checked
  and filed separately afterwards.

One file, so the lead implements it.
