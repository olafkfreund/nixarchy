# Intent: a nixpkgs bump's system jobs finish well inside their limits

## Problem

On #1457, a nixpkgs bump, `system-build` took 56 minutes and
`system-checks` 60. Each job runs its steps one after another, on one 4-core
hosted runner. Most of the time is cold compiles of our own derivations,
which Hydra never has:

- `quickshell`, our patched override: 21 minutes;
- `gliff`: 17 minutes;
- `owe`, `omasnap` and others.

The pull request pushes none of it, so `main` compiles all of it again after
the merge, and so does every re-run. Steps that do not depend on each other
still wait for each other:

- the session VM test (10 minutes);
- the allowlisted tools and box image (11 minutes);
- the cache-budget step (7 minutes). Like #1460, it evaluates one attribute
  per process.

## Proposed outcome

- **A bump's own CI warms the cache.** A same-repository `update/nixpkgs-*`
  pull request pushes the cache-allowlist closures, minus every path that
  depends on the flake source (paths that only that commit would ever ask
  for). `main` after the merge, and every re-run, downloads what the bump
  already compiled.
- **The session VM test and the allowlisted-tools build run as their own
  job** in parallel, under the existing `system` aggregator. The required
  contexts are unchanged.
- **The cache-budget step builds and measures the allowlist in one
  evaluation.**

## Affected users and systems

CI only: `cachix-push.sh`, `cache-budget.sh`, `build.yml`'s system jobs and
aggregator, and `tests/cache-budget.nix`. nixarchy.cachix.org receives
allowlisted paths earlier than before; it receives no new kinds of path.

## Constraints

- **The owner approved pushes from bump branches,** a change to the #697
  rule, for allowlist entries only, inside `cache-budget.sh`'s budget.
- **Fork PRs push nothing.** They have no secret, and the script also checks
  the head repository.
- **The aggregator must fail when any of its jobs fails** (#1341, #1134), the
  new one included: it lists its jobs by hand (§4).
- **Each guard is shown to fail (§1).**

## Open questions

None. Running these jobs on p620's shared store is a measured second phase,
filed separately if wanted.
