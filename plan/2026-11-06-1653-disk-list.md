# Plan: The installer's disk list opens on an internal disk

## Approved decisions

- New function `disk_rows` in `installer/install.sh` prints the rows `ask_device` offers, internal disks first. `gum choose` opens on the first row; nothing else about the menu changes.
- A disk is removable when `lsblk -dnro RM,TRAN <dev>` gives `RM=1` or `TRAN=usb`. `mmc` is not a signal. A disk `lsblk` can't answer for counts as internal.
- Each group keeps `lsblk`'s order. The row format, the zram/floppy/size filters, the boot-medium exclusion and their comments move into `disk_rows` unchanged.
- `ask_device` keeps the empty-list refusal, `gum choose` and `remember_disk`.
- Out of scope: 4096-byte LUKS sectors, the old RAID/VG test (recorded on #1610).
- #1649 reuses `disk_rows`. Both land as one PR, one commit per issue, merge commit; this commit first.

## Steps

The loop sits on the right of a pipeline and so in a subshell: wrap the `while` and the final printing in one `{ ...; }` group on that side of the pipe, so the two variables are printed where they were filled. `ask_device` does `list=$(disk_rows)`.
