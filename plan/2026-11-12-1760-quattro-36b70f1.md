## Rollback

- **Before promotion.** The carry sits on `edge-candidate` as human commits.
  `edge-carry.sh` keeps them for the next run, and stops runs with
  `candidate-diverged` once `origin/edge` moves past them. A later run never
  replaces them. To back out: ask the owner, then delete `edge-candidate`
  (or reset it to `origin/edge`); both are force operations on a shared
  branch (§11).
- **After promotion.** `edge` moves only through `edge-promote.sh`. Back out by
  a new carry on `edge-candidate` that reverts this carry's commits and pins
  `ceb30ed` again (url, `omarchyVersion`, lock), dispatched with
  `from_candidate=true`, so promotion runs the checks again. p620 and razer
  then deploy that. A NixOS rollback (`nixos-rebuild switch --rollback`) undoes
  the system only: what migrations and setters already wrote in `$HOME` stays
  (the dconf icon theme, `qtengine/config.json`, `.bak` files and
  `~/.agents/AGENTS.md` links, the migration markers). Those are restored by
  hand, from the `.bak` files where there are some.
- `main` changes only through the artifacts PR.
